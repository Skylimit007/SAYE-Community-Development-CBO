About the organisation

A community organisation rooted in service and practical development.

Who we are

SAYE Community Development CBO is a locally registered organisation focused on uplifting the community through productive farming, environmental stewardship, sanitation, and social support initiatives. The organisation exists to bring people together, support vulnerable households, and create sustainable opportunities for better livelihoods.

“Together, we grow stronger communities through cooperation, dignity, and practical action.”

Mission

To improve the welfare of community members through sustainable development activities, environmental conservation, sanitation, and support programs that strengthen households and livelihoods.

Vision

A self-reliant, healthy, and empowered community where people work together to create sustainable livelihoods and protect the wellbeing of vulnerable groups.

Programs and focus areas

What SAYE Community Development CBO does

Tree Nurseries & Agroforestry

Poultry & Livestock Support

Encouraging income generation and food security through poultry keeping and related livestock production activities.

🐝

Beekeeping

Supporting practical beekeeping initiatives that can improve household livelihoods and strengthen local enterprise.

🦗

Cricket Farming

Exploring innovative farming approaches that support nutrition, enterprise development, and economic empowerment.

💧

Water & Sanitation

Advancing healthier communities through improved sanitation awareness, safe water practices, and hygiene-focused initiatives.

🤝

Support for Vulnerable People

Assisting vulnerable children, elderly persons, and other community members who need social care and practical support.
